Write or edit the following copy: $ARGUMENTS

## Voice

Plain English. Short sentences. Specific to Rhema Real Estates, not any land agency.

## What to never write

- "Discover", "Elevate", "Unlock", "Seamless", "Curated experience", "Your journey".
- Em dashes (—).
- Eyebrow tags (the small all-caps label above a heading).
- Emojis.
- Sentences that could belong to any real estate site if you swap the company name.

## What to write instead

- About a plot: what it is, where it is, who it suits.
  "A quarter-acre in Kamakis, five minutes from the Eastern Bypass. Suitable for a family home or a small rental block."
- About a region: one concrete detail.
  "Ruiru is on the Thika Road corridor, with commuter rail access and a growing retail centre."
- About the company: what Rhema does, not what Rhema feels.
  "Rhema Real Estates sells land in Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, and the Thika Road corridor."
- Button labels: "View plot", "Enquire", "See all plots in Ruiru".
  Never "Discover more" or "Start your journey".
- Phone and WhatsApp: 0711634163. Show it where a buyer would look for it, not as a banner.

## Test before returning copy

Ask: if I replaced "Rhema Real Estates" with any other land agency, would this sentence still make sense? If yes, rewrite it.
